# A three-minute Trajectree demo

Open the live application before your turn and keep the browser at 100% zoom. Use fictional data throughout. Demo settings lets you restore the sample academy if you want a clean starting point.

1. **The problem, 20 seconds.** Open the overview. Say: “Grassroots coaches have the players and the commitment. Trajectree gives their development work a structure, with one connected record for every player.” Point to assessed players, approved plans and upcoming sessions.
2. **Understand the individual, 30 seconds.** Open Arjun Mehta's player passport and then Assessments. Show the four dimensions and coach observations. Say: “The coach records the assessment. The platform organises the information; it does not make the selection decision.”
3. **Give practice a direction, 40 seconds.** Open Arjun's Receive with purpose plan. Show baseline 12, target 24, the same 30-receive protocol and the coach's approval. Open First-touch builders in Development clusters. Explain that shared practice needs can be grouped while targets stay individual.
4. **Close the loop, 50 seconds.** Open Receiving circuit & wall passes in Interventions. Update the session to Completed and record observations. In Arjun's plan, choose Record progress and enter 22, using the same measurement protocol. Show the resulting 83% progress toward the target. Refresh, reopen his passport and show the saved measurement and session.
5. **Explain the business, 40 seconds.** Open Demo settings to show proposed 12-week tiers: ₹699, ₹999 and ₹1,499 per player. Say: “These are proposed prices to test with academies. This prototype demonstrates the workflow; the business case must establish adoption, delivery cost and willingness to pay.”

## Be precise with judges

- The current app uses fictional data and browser storage. A shared academy database, real user permissions and validated assessment protocols are future implementation work.
- Present observed improvement as recorded progress, not evidence that Trajectree caused it.
- Explain who approves a plan, who delivers the session and how the next assessment changes the player's pathway.
- Use the prototype to support the story. Your evidence, economics and answers to the judges still determine the strength of the competition entry.
